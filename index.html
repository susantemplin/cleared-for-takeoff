What they're listening for

Reviewing your answer… this can take up to a minute.

Interviewers ask for real examples, and they can come from any job, school, volunteering, or family life. Build a few strong stories once, and you can bend them to fit almost any question.

Where were you and what was going on?
What was your job in that moment?
What did you do, step by step? Say "I," not "we."
How did it end? Numbers help: minutes saved, people helped, a thank-you.
